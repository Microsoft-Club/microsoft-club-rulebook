# MICROSOFT CLUB GIKI
## Active Club Roster & Operational State

*This living document serves as the official, dynamic record of active wings as mandated by Article IV of the Microsoft Club GIKI Rulebook. It is managed and updated by the current Executive Committee.*

---

### 1. Official Wings
*Modifying the roster of Official Wings requires a 2/3 supermajority vote of the Executive Committee, as these represent the permanent operational pillars of the Club.*

* **Development Wing:** Builds software projects. (Reports to Dev Director)
* **L&D Wing:** Delivers technical sessions. (Reports to Program Manager)
* **Logikal:** Produces podcast episodes. (Reports to Program Manager)
* **Marketing Wing:** Manages digital presence. (Reports to Program Manager)
* **Outreach Wing:** Secures sponsorships. (Reports to Program Manager)

### 2. Provisional Wings
*Modifying the roster of Provisional Wings requires a simple majority vote of the Executive Committee. These wings are currently undergoing a mandatory 2-year probationary cycle to prove operational sustainability.*

* **Competitive Engagement Wing:** Trains teams for hackathons. *(Current Status: Undergoing 2-Year Cycle)*
* **Brainees (Research Wing):** Fosters academic paper writing. *(Current Status: Year 1 of 2)*
